# Get Support

If you've worked through the relevant troubleshooting steps and still have a problem, here's how to reach us.

---

## Before you contact support

Check the [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) page first. It covers the most common issues and is faster than waiting for a reply.

Also check:
- The manager's own status messages. Hover any status badge or control for a tooltip with more detail.
- The GitHub releases page for known issues in the current version.

---

## Contact

**Email:** support@invictuscockpits.com

**Website:** [invictuscockpits.com](https://invictuscockpits.com)

When you write in, include:
- Your **manager version** (shown in Settings → About, or in the title bar)
- Your **board type** (Sidewinder or Phoenix) and **firmware version** (shown on the Firmware page)
- Which **sim** you're using (DCS, BMS, or both) and the version
- A description of what you expected to happen and what actually happened
- Any error messages. Copy the text exactly, or take a screenshot

---

## Reporting a bug

If you found a repeatable bug in the manager software, open an issue on the releases repository:

[github.com/invictuscockpits/aim-cockpit-manager-releases/issues](https://github.com/invictuscockpits/aim-cockpit-manager-releases/issues)

Include the same information as above, plus steps to reproduce.
