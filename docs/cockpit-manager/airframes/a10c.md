# A-10C

Every panel the manager knows on the A-10C, in the order the Console Panels page lists them: what the panel is, then each switch, knob, lamp and gauge on it, what its positions do, and what it takes to wire. The Docs button on a panel in the manager opens that panel's section on this page.

> [!NOTE]
> This airframe was generated from the simulator's own cockpit data. Panels marked **draft** have not been checked against a real cockpit yet, so names and grouping can still change. Wiring you record against a draft panel is kept when it is renamed.

Controls: 449. Panels: 47.

## Left Console

### ANTI G

*Draft: generated from the simulator, not yet checked against a real cockpit.*

Test button for the anti-G suit valve.

**ANTI G TEST** · Push button · 1 pin<br>
ANTI-G SUIT VALVE TEST

Hold to inflate the G-suit on the ground so the fit and the valve can be
checked before flight. Releases as soon as the button is let go.

### KY58 RCU

*Draft: generated from the simulator, not yet checked against a real cockpit.*

KY-58 remote control unit: zeroize, delay, radio and fill selectors, mode and power.

**ZEROIZE** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin

Lift the cover and select Z to wipe every loaded crypto key. There is no
undo: the keys have to be reloaded on the ground.

**DELAY** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin

Holds the crypto keys in memory for a short time after power is removed,
so a brief interruption does not wipe them.

**C/RAD SELECT** · Rotary selector · 3 pins, or 1 analog input<br>
PLAIN / CIPHER RADIO SELECT

**PLAIN:** Centre detent. Plain voice, no encryption.
**C/RAD 1:** Cipher on the first radio.
**C/RAD 2:** Cipher on the second radio.

**FILL SELECT** · Rotary selector · 6 pins, or 1 analog input

Picks which of the six loaded crypto key positions is in use. Numbered
1 through 6 clockwise around the knob.

**MODE** · Rotary selector · 3 pins, or 1 analog input

**OP:** Operate, normal secure voice.
**LD:** Load, for loading a crypto key from a fill device.
**RV:** Receive variable, for taking a key over the air.

**POWER** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
KY-58 POWER

Powers the secure voice unit. The KY-58 is not modeled in DCS, so every
control on this panel moves but has no effect in the sim.

### LASTE

*Draft: generated from the simulator, not yet checked against a real cockpit.*

LASTE panel: autopilot mode and engage, enhanced attitude control and radar altimeter.

**AUTOPILOT MODE** · 3-position toggle · 2 pins<br>
LOW ALTITUDE AUTOPILOT MODE

**ALT:** Holds the current bank angle and altitude.
**ALT/HDG:** Holds the current altitude and heading.
**PATH:** Holds the current flight path.

**AUTOPILOT ENGAGE** · Push button · 1 pin<br>
AUTOPILOT ENGAGE / DISENGAGE

Push to engage the autopilot in the selected mode, push again to drop

**EAC** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
ENHANCED ATTITUDE CONTROL

**RDR ALTM** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
RADAR ALTIMETER

**NRM:** Radar altimeter powered and feeding the altitude readout and
the low-altitude warning.
**DIS:** Disabled, which also stops it radiating.

Starts the auxiliary power unit, which supplies the bleed air and

**FLAPS** · 3-position toggle · 2 pins<br>
