### MANUAL TRIM

Trim wheels and position indicators for roll, pitch and yaw, and the switch that disconnects trim and the autopilot.

**ROLL TRIM** · Motorized potentiometer · 1 analog input<br>
MANUAL ROLL TRIM WHEEL

Adjusts roll-axis trim. Rotate top edge LEFT to drop the left wing; right to drop the right wing. Spring-centered detent at 0 = no trim.

**Range:** -1.0 (full left-wing-down) to +1.0 (full right-wing-down), normalized.

**Motor-driven:** The wheel is a Bourns PRM162-K415K-503A2 motor pot — when the sim moves the trim arg (autopilot trim, electrical trim, mission start), the wheel auto-positions to match.

**PITCH TRIM** · Motorized potentiometer · 1 analog input<br>
MANUAL PITCH TRIM WHEEL

Adjusts pitch-axis trim. Roll wheel forward to trim nose down; back for nose up. Spring-centered detent at 0.

**Range:** -1.0 (full nose-down) to +1.0 (full nose-up), normalized.

**Motor-driven:** Wheel auto-positions when the sim updates the trim arg via autopilot pitch hold or electrical trim from the side-stick.

**YAW TRIM** · Potentiometer with detent · 1 analog input<br>
MANUAL YAW TRIM KNOB

Adjusts yaw-axis trim. Rotate clockwise (right) to trim nose right; counter-clockwise (left) for nose-left trim. Mechanical center detent at 0, marked at 3 o'clock.

**Range:** -1.0 (full left-yaw, pointer at 12 o'clock) to +1.0 (full right-yaw, pointer at 6 o'clock), normalized.

**Hardware:** Standard manual potentiometer (not motor-driven). Smaller travel than the pitch/roll wheels — the F-16 needs less yaw trim authority. The pilot sets it once for trimmed flight; sim doesn't push values back to it.

**ROLL TRIM IND** · Needle gauge · display<br>
ROLL TRIM POSITION INDICATOR

Switec X-27.168 stepper-driven needle on a half-moon dial showing current roll-axis trim. Needle hangs from the chord at the top of the gauge and sweeps the lower arc.

**Range:** -1.0 (needle full left = full left-wing-down) to +1.0 (needle full right = full right-wing-down). Center mark at the bottom of the arc = neutral trim.

Read-only — the indicator follows the trim arg even when trim is changed via electrical-trim or autopilot, not just the manual wheel.

**PITCH TRIM IND** · Needle gauge · display<br>
PITCH TRIM POSITION INDICATOR

Switec X-27.168 stepper-driven needle on a half-moon dial showing current pitch-axis trim. Same geometry as the roll indicator — chord at top, curve below.

**Range:** -1.0 (needle full left = full nose-down) to +1.0 (needle full right = full nose-up). Center mark = neutral trim.

Read-only — the indicator follows the trim arg regardless of source (manual wheel, electrical trim, autopilot).

**TRIM/AP DISC** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
TRIM/AUTOPILOT DISCONNECT SWITCH

- **DISC**: Disconnects the autopilot AND disables electrical pitch trim — only manual mechanical pitch trim remains available.
- **NORM**: Normal autopilot and electrical-trim operation (default).

**Emergency Use:** Snap to DISC to recover from runaway trim or an autopilot hardover. Adjacent to the trim wheels for fast access in a runaway-trim scenario.

### FLT CONTROL

Flight control panel: alternate flaps, digital backup, manual terrain-following fly-up, leading-edge flaps, and the FLCS built-in test with its reset.

**ALT FLAPS** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
ALTERNATE FLAPS SWITCH

- **EXTEND**: Manually extends leading- and trailing-edge flaps via the alternate-flaps system, bypassing the FLCS-controlled flap schedule.
- **NORM**: Flaps under FLCS control (default).

Used when normal flap operation has failed. Aircraft must be slowed before extending.

**DIGITAL BACKUP** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
DIGITAL BACKUP (DBU) SWITCH

- **BACKUP**: Selects the backup FLCS software image, bypassing the optional advanced control laws.
- **OFF**: Normal FLCS operation (default).

Used as a workaround for FLCS faults. Standard procedure recommends using BACKUP only on advice from technical orders.

**MANUAL TF FLYUP** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
MANUAL TERRAIN-FOLLOWING FLYUP SWITCH

- **ENABLE**: Activates manual TF FLYUP — autopilot pulls up if terrain rises faster than the aircraft can climb.
- **DISABLE**: FLYUP protection disabled (default).

Use only with TF radar in operation. The system commands a 4G pull-up to clear obstacles when terrain conflict is detected.

**LE FLAPS** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
LEADING EDGE FLAPS SWITCH

- **LOCK**: Mechanically locks the leading-edge flaps in their current position.
- **AUTO**: LEF position scheduled by the FLCS based on AoA and Mach (default).

Used if an LEF malfunction is detected. Locking the LEFs increases minimum approach speed.

**BIT IND** · 2 lamps · output<br>
FLCS BIT INDICATOR

Two-cell status indicator alongside the FLCS BIT switch.

**RUN (green, top):** Illuminates while a Flight Control System Built-In Test is in progress. Extinguishes when BIT completes successfully.

**FAIL (red, bottom):** Illuminates if the Built-In Test detects a fault. Stays on until cleared via the FLCS RESET switch (assuming the underlying fault has resolved).

**FLCS BIT** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
FLCS BIT SWITCH

Magnetically-held toggle. Flipping up to BIT initiates the Flight Control System Built-In Test; the magnet holds the lever in place during the ~45 s test cycle and releases automatically when BIT completes (or fails), dropping the switch to OFF.

**BIT Cycle:** The aircraft cycles through control-surface movement to verify FLCS integrity. The BIT RUN light (green) illuminates during the test; the BIT FAIL light (red) illuminates if any fault is detected.

**Caution:** Do not initiate during flight or with any control input. Aircraft must be stationary on the ground.

**FLCS RESET** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
FLCS RESET SWITCH

Spring-loaded toggle. Hold up to RESET to clear latched FLCS faults; the lever then springs back to OFF.

**Use:** After a transient FLCS fault, flip up to RESET to attempt fault clearance. If the FLCS warning illuminates again, the fault is persistent.

### IFF

IFF control panel: master mode, Mode 4 code handling, replies and monitoring, and the C&I and enable switches.

**MASTER** · Rotary selector · 5 pins, or 1 analog input<br>
IFF MASTER Knob — OFF/STBY/LOW/NORM/EMER

**OFF:** Transponder unpowered.
**STBY:** Powered, no replies.
**LOW:** Reduced transmit power (close-in formation flight).
**NORM:** Full-power normal operation.
**EMER:** Emergency — transmits Mode 3 squawk 7700 and Mode 1
emergency code regardless of selector positions.

**M-4 CODE** · 3-position toggle · 2 pins<br>
IFF M-4 CODE Switch — HOLD/A·B/ZERO

**HOLD (down, momentary):** Maintains the active Mode 4 crypto
key through the daily key change, suppressing the auto-zero.
**A/B (center):** Normal operating position — uses the loaded
Mode 4 codes A and B.
**ZERO (up, momentary):** Zeroises the Mode 4 codes from the
transponder.

**C&I** · Rotary selector · 2 pins, or 1 analog input<br>
IFF C & I Knob — UFC/BACKUP

**UFC:** Mode codes are entered via the Up-Front Controller (DED).
**BACKUP:** Mode codes are set by the four selector levers in the
MODE PACK on this panel — used when the UFC has failed.

**ENABLE** · 3-position toggle · 2 pins<br>
IFF ENABLE Switch — M1/M3, OFF, M3/MS

**M1/M3 (down):** Enable Mode 1 and Mode 3 replies.
**OFF (center):** All replies disabled (Mode 1/3/S off).
**M3/MS (up):** Enable Mode 3 and Mode S replies.

**IFF MODE** · IFF mode levers · 1 pin<br>
IFF MODE Selector Pack — M-1 D1 / M-1 D2 / M-3 D1 / M-3 D2

Four springloaded selector levers, each with a 0-9 digit drum
above. Flick a lever UP to increment or DOWN to decrement the drum
above it. Used in BACKUP mode when the UFC isn't driving the IFF
directly.

**MODE 4 REPLY** · 3-position toggle · 2 pins<br>
IFF MODE 4 REPLY Switch — OUT/A/B

**OUT:** Mode 4 disabled — transponder will not reply to Mode
4 interrogations.
**A:** Reply with Mode 4 code A.
**B:** Reply with Mode 4 code B.

**MODE 4 MONITOR** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
IFF MODE 4 MONITOR Switch — OUT/AUDIO

**OUT:** No Mode 4 reply audio in headset.
**AUDIO:** Audio tone alert in headset on every Mode 4 challenge.

### EPU

Emergency power unit: the EPU switch and its run indicators.

**EPU IND** · 2 lamps · output<br>
EPU FUEL-SOURCE INDICATOR

**HYDRAZN (yellow, top):** EPU is drawing on the H-70 hydrazine
tank — normal operating mode when the EPU has been triggered.

**AIR (yellow, bottom):** EPU has dropped to air-only operation

**EPU** · Lamp · output<br>
EPU RUN LIGHT

Green push-to-test indicator. Illuminates while the Emergency Power
Unit is running and providing electrical/hydraulic output. Push the
cell to verify lamp integrity.

**EPU** · 3-position toggle · 2 pins<br>
EPU SWITCH — OFF/NORM/ON

Three-position switch with red/yellow guards over both OFF and ON;
NORM is the unguarded resting center.

**OFF (down, guarded):** EPU disabled.
**NORM (center):** EPU armed — auto-starts on loss of main
electrical or hydraulic pressure.
**ON (up, guarded):** EPU manually commanded to run regardless
of system status.

### ENGINE START

Engine start panel: jet fuel starter, engine control, afterburner reset and max power.

**RUN** · Lamp · output<br>
JFS RUN LIGHT

Green dome. Illuminates while the Jet Fuel Starter is spinning at
operating RPM. Used as the magnet-release trigger for the JFS
selector lever — once RUN comes on, the magnet drops and the JFS
lever springs back to OFF, leaving the JFS to keep itself running
on its own combustor.

**JFS** · 3-position toggle, spring return · 2 pins<br>
JFS SWITCH — START 1 / OFF / START 2

Magnetically-held selector for the Jet Fuel Starter. Pull to
START 1 (right hydraulic accumulator) or START 2 (both accumulators,
backup) to spin the engine; the magnet holds the lever until JFS
RUN illuminates, then drops the lever back to OFF.

**ENG CONT** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
ENGINE CONTROL SWITCH — PRI / SEC

Guarded 2-pos toggle (red guard, NORMALLY in PRI).
**PRI:** Primary digital engine control (DEEC) — normal flight.
**SEC:** Secondary backup hydromechanical control. Used when the
DEEC fails or for ground troubleshooting. Performance is degraded.

**AB RESET** · 3-position toggle, spring return · 2 pins<br>
AB RESET / ENG DATA SWITCH

Spring-loaded 3-pos:
**AB RESET (up, momentary):** Resets latched afterburner faults
in the DEEC.
**NORM (center):** Resting position — normal operation.
**ENG DATA (down, momentary):** Triggers an engine-data dump for
maintenance crews.

Not modeled in BMS.

**MAX POWER** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
MAX POWER SWITCH

Guarded 2-pos toggle. Non-functional on F-16C/D Block 50/52 (GE F110
variant) — present in the cockpit for parts commonality with the
P&W F100 panel where it commands maximum unrestricted power. The
switch is also non-functional in DCS.

### MANUAL PITCH

Manual pitch override switch for recovering from a deep stall.

**MANUAL PITCH** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
MANUAL PITCH OVERRIDE SWITCH

- **OVRD**: Bypasses the FLCS pitch-axis limiter, restoring full pitch authority for deep-stall recovery.
- **NORM**: FLCS pitch limiter active (default; protects against departure).

**Use:** Hold OVRD only as needed to break a deep stall. The switch is spring-loaded to NORM — releasing returns the limiter to active.

### ENGINE INSTRUMENTS

Engine instruments: oil pressure, nozzle position, RPM, FTIT and fuel flow.

**OIL** · Needle gauge · display<br>
ENGINE OIL PRESSURE

Displays engine oil pressure in PSI. Normal range at military power
is 30-65 PSI; idle is typically 15-25 PSI.

**Warning (red arc <15 PSI):** Oil starvation, imminent engine
damage. Reduce throttle to idle, prepare for engine flame-out, and
follow the OIL LOSS / OIL PRESSURE LOW checklist.

An OIL HOT caution light may accompany abnormally low or high pressure
readings.

**NOZ** · Needle gauge · display<br>
ENGINE NOZZLE POSITION

Indicates exhaust nozzle aperture as a percentage of fully open.

**Idle:** ~100% (wide open for low core RPM).
**Military:** ~0-5% (closed, maximum dry thrust).
**Afterburner:** opens progressively with reheat segments (5-AB through
max AB) to maintain optimal exhaust velocity.

Reading should track throttle commands smoothly. A nozzle stuck open
in MIL produces a STORES CONFIG-like thrust shortfall; closed in idle
indicates a control-system failure.

**RPM** · Needle gauge · display<br>
ENGINE TACHOMETER

Indicates engine rotor RPM as a percentage of maximum rated speed.

**Normal operation:** idle ≈ 65%, military ≈ 96%, max afterburner ≈ 100%.
**Redline:** 102% continuous limit (no face mark, documented in dash-1).

A reading stuck near 0 in flight indicates a flame-out — initiate
airstart procedures per the dash-1 EP checklist.

**FTIT** · Needle gauge · display<br>
FORWARD TURBINE INLET TEMPERATURE (FTIT)

Measures turbine inlet temperature in degrees Celsius (×100). Critical
indicator of engine health and protection against overtemperature
damage during start, military, and afterburner operation.

**Caution band (yellow):** 935-979 °C — engine is hot, monitor.
**Warning band (red):** ≥980 °C — immediate action required, retard
throttle, follow ENGINE OVERHEAT checklist.

Start hot-light occurs around 935 °C during engine start; abort start
if FTIT continues to climb past 980 °C.

FUEL FLOW INDICATOR

- **TEST MASK**: (Momentary down) Tests mask seal and oxygen flow. Hold down and inhale - pressure should build if seal is good
- **NORMAL**: Normal position for flight operations
- **EMERGENCY**: Provides continuous positive pressure oxygen flow for emergency situations (smoke, fumes, rapid decompression)

Not modeled in BMS.

**DILUTER LEVER** · 2-position toggle · 1 pin<br>
OXYGEN DILUTER LEVER

- **NORM**: Normal dilution mode. Oxygen mixed with air based on altitude (diluter-demand). Use for normal operations below 30,000 feet
- **100%**: Pure oxygen, no air dilution. Use above 30,000 feet or if smoke/fumes present in cockpit

Not modeled in BMS.

**SUPPLY LEVER** · 3-position toggle · 2 pins<br>
OXYGEN SUPPLY LEVER

- **OFF**: Closes oxygen supply valve. Use only on the ground for maintenance or when switching to emergency oxygen bottle
- **ON**: Normal position. Opens oxygen supply valve for normal oxygen flow from aircraft system
- **PBG**: Pressure Breathing for G. Provides slight positive pressure to assist with high-G breathing and reduce G-induced fatigue. Typically used during high-G maneuvering

The PBG position is not modeled in BMS.
